Welcome to MemMoji

MemMoji is a memory card matching game. Your goal is simple: find all the matching pairs of emoji cards hidden on the board. Flip two cards — if they match, they are removed from the board. If they don't, they flip back and you try again.

Sounds easy? It is at first! But as you level up, the boards grow larger and cards can display multiple emoji that must be matched exactly. You'll need a sharp memory to keep up.

MemMoji is a single-player game. Your progress is saved automatically when you complete levels and change settings.
