Score System

MemMoji tracks four categories of moves to show how well you're using your memory. These aren't point scores — they're performance metrics that help you understand your play style. The only competitive measure is your completion time, which feeds into high scores (see Hints & High Scores).

Remembered

You matched two cards, and the second card was one you had already flipped earlier in this level. This means your memory is working — you saw it before, you remembered where it was, and you went back for it. On the level complete screen, these pairs light up green.

Guess

You matched two cards, but the second card had never been flipped before in this level. You got lucky! Both cards are gone, which is great, but it didn't require any memory. On the level complete screen, these pairs light up gold/yellow.

Forgot

You flipped two non-matching cards, but you had already seen the matching card for one of your picks earlier in the level. You saw where it was but didn't connect it to your current pick. Work on this: if you're getting lots of Forgets, slow down and think before picking.

Miss

You flipped two non-matching cards, and there was no visible matching information available. This is expected, especially early in a level before you've seen many cards. Misses aren't bad — you're gathering information!

Stats Tables

The stats panel shows two tables during play:

  • Level Stats — counts just for the current level (resets each level)
  • Session Stats — running total since you started playing (resets when you click New Game or jump to a lower level)

The Levels column in Session Stats shows how many levels you've completed this session.

These metrics are for the current session only — they are not saved between play sessions. The only persistent record is your completion time on the high score board.

Improving Your Play

  • Ideal play: all Remembered, no Forgot, minimal Miss
  • Reducing Forgot moves means you're making better use of your memory
  • Reducing Miss moves means you're being strategic about which cards to try
  • Faster completion time = higher ranking on the high score board
Watched a card get flipped and want to find its match? Click it first! If you know where its pair is, that becomes a Remembered match — and you'll clear the board faster.
